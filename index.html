Hexa Tower

Spin up a neon hexagon tower to the beat.

Hexa Tower is a neon rhythm game and endless runner in one. Your ship spins its way up a glowing hexagon tower while walls rush towards you on the beat of the music. Slip through the gaps, learn the patterns and see how far you can fly before the speed gets the better of you.
