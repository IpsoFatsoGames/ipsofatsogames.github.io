Hexa Tower

Spin up a neon hexagon tower to the beat.

Hold left, hold right, and don't touch the walls.

Hexa Tower is a neon rhythm game and endless runner in one. Your ship spins its way up a glowing hexagon tower while walls rush towards you on the beat of the music. Slip through the gaps, learn the patterns and see how far you can fly before the speed gets the better of you.

Features

  • Five endless modes, from Chill to Hyperspace, each with its own speed, music and global leaderboard
  • Every wall lands on the beat of a retro arcade and chiptune soundtrack
  • Simple hold-left, hold-right controls: easy to learn, hard to master
  • Short runs, perfect for a quick break
  • Synthwave neon visuals under a retro sunset sky
